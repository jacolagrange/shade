How to play

  1. 1

    Place exactly one tree in every row, every column and every colored region.

  2. 2

    Each tree casts a diagonal shadow away from the sun. It runs through the rest of its own region, spills into the next region, and stops at that region's edge.

  3. 3

    No tree may stand in another tree's shadow.

  4. 4

    Every picnic blanket must end up in shade.

Tap once to mark a cell as empty, twice for a tree. Drag to mark several cells. The sun moves every day, and weekends have two.
